Integration of Rajasthan

At the time of independence Rajasthan had :-
- 19 Princely states.
- 3 Independent Chieftawnships: Neemrana (Alwar), Lawa (Tonk), Kushalgarh (Banswara)
- 1 Centrally administered territory

Initial Attempts:
- Mewar Maharana Bhupal Singh invited Kota, Bundi, Jhalawar to form Hadoti Union.
- Dungarpur Maharawal Laxman Singh invited Dungarpur, Banswara, Pratapgarh to form Wagad Union.
- All this resulted in failure due to clash of ego among kings.

Secretariat of Princely States :-
- Foundation: 5th July, 1947
- Head: Sardar Vallabh Bhai Patel
- Secretary: V.P. Menon
- He was given the Herculean task to convince all princely states (P.S.)
- Secretariat announced that to remain independent P.S. have to have 1 crore income and 10 Lakh population.
- Only 4 P.S. in Rajasthan had this requirement: Mewar, Marwar, Jaipur, Bikaner.
- On 18th July 1947 Indian independence act was passed and under its section 8 Supremacy of British on princely states was ended.
- On August 7, 1947, Bikaner Maharaja Sardul Singh Signed the instrument of Accession. (First in Rajasthan)
- On August 14, 1947 Maharaja Udaybhan Singh of Dholpur had signed the merger later. (Last in Rajasthan)

Rajasthan was integrated in 7 phases (1948-1956)

Phase I :- Matsya Union :-
- Named by K.M. Munshi, From: Mahabharat: Matsya Janpad.
- States included: Dholpur, Karauli, Alwar, Bharatpur + Neemrana (Thikana)
- Udaibhan Singh (Dholpur) - Raj Pramukh
- Ganeshpal (Karauli) - Deputy Raj Pramukh
- Capital: Alwar
- Inauguration: Narhari Vishnu Gadgil on 18 March, 1948 AD
- Prime Minister: Shobaram Kumawat (Alwar)
- Deputy P.M.: Jugal Kishore Chaturvedi (Bharatpur) - "Nehru of Rajasthan"
- Other Ministers: Master Bholanath (Alwar), Gopilal Yadav (Bharatpur), Dr. Mangal Singh (Dholpur), Chiranjilal Sharma (Karauli)
- At the time of formation income: 1.84 Crores, Population: 18 Lakhs
- S.N. Sapru: Administrator of Bharatpur.
- Note: Meeting Attended 1st Round Table conference (1930 London)
  * Alwar: Dr. V.B. Kharve (PM)
  * Bharatpur: Tej Singh (King)
  *(accused in assassination of Gandhi; Govt. of India removed them and took control)

Phase II. Rajasthan Union/Eastern Rajasthan Union
- States included: Kota, Bundi, Jhalawar, Dungarpur, Banswara, Pratapgarh, Tonk, Kishangarh, Shahpura + Kushalgarh (Thikana)
- Bhim Singh (Kota) - Rajpramukh
- Bahadur Singh (Bundi) - Senior Deputy Rajpramukh
- Lakshman Singh (Dungarpur) - Junior Deputy Rajpramukh
- Capital: Kota
- Inauguration: 25th March 1948 by Gadgil.
- Kishangarh & Shahpura (Smallest) opposed merger; Ajmer-Merwara canon salutes were not allowed to these princely states.
- Banswara's Chandraveer Singh exclaimed while signing instrument of Accession: "I am signing my death warrant."

Phase III. United States of Rajasthan (Rajasthan Union + Mewar)
- Inauguration: 18th April 1948 by Jawaharlal Nehru at Udaipur.
- Rajpramukh: Maharana Bhupal Singh (Udaipur)
- Sr. Deputy Rajpramukh: Bheem Singh (Kota)
- Jr. Deputy Rajpramukh: Bahadur Singh (Bundi), Laxman Singh (Dungarpur)
- Prime Minister: Manikya Lal Verma.
- Deputy Prime Minister: Gokul Lal Asawa.
- Capital: Udaipur.
- Decided that 1st session of the legislative assembly would take place in Kota.
- Special efforts would be made for development of Kota like establishment of:
  1. Head Office of Commissioner
  2. Police training College
  3. Aeronautical College.
- Mewar Maharana Bhupal Singh demanded privy purse of Rs. 20 Lacs per annum:
  * 10 lakh: Official Privy Purse
  * 5 lakh: As salary of Raj Pramukh
  * 5 lakh: As religious grants

Rajasthan Andolan Samiti/Rajasthan Movement Committee:
- Founded by Ram Manohar Lohiya
- Objective: To raise voice for the unification of entire Rajasthan on National level.
- Later on Ram Manohar Lohiya also demanded that Matsya Union should be merged with Greater Rajasthan in order to form a Greater Rajasthan.
- Note: Maharana Bhupal Singh of Mewar tried to form Rajasthan Union by joining the princely states of Rajputana, Malwa and Saurashtra. For this, two conferences were organized in Udaipur (1946, 1947 AD).
- K.M. Munshi was the constitutional advisor of Mewar Maharana.

Phase IV. Greater Rajasthan
- States included: United States of Rajasthan + Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer.
- Inauguration: 30th March 1949 AD by Vallabh Bhai Patel at Jaipur.
- (30th March is celebrated as "Rajasthan Diwas" - First bright day of Chaitra Vikram Samvat 2006).
- Maharaj Pramukh: Maharana Bhupal Singh.
- Rajpramukh: Sawai Mansingh-II (Jaipur)
- Senior Deputy Rajpramukh: Bheem Singh (Kota), Hanuwant Singh (Jodhpur)
- Jr. Deputy Rajpramukh: Bahadur Singh (Bundi), Laxman Singh (Dungarpur)
- Prime Minister: Heera Lal Shastri.
- Privy Purse:
  * Jaipur: 18 lac
  * Jodhpur: 17.5 lac
  * Bikaner: 17 lac
- Dispute between Jaipur and Jodhpur for capital. To resolve it, a committee was formed (Members: B.R. Patel, T.C. Puri, S.P. Sinha).
- On recommendation of this committee, Jaipur was made capital and High Court was given to Jodhpur.
- On 19th July 1948: Lawa Thikana was merged in Jaipur princely state.

Phase V :- United States of Greater Rajasthan
- Greater Rajasthan + Matsya Union (14 + 2 states, 4 + 1 thikanas).
- Merged on 15th May 1949 AD on basis of recommendation of Shankar Rao Dev committee.
- Sobha Ram Kumawat was included in Heera Lal Shastri Cabinet.

Phase VI
- 89 villages including Abu and Delwara were merged in Bombay.
- Rest of Sirohi was merged in Rajasthan including "Hathal" village (birthplace of Gokul Bhai Bhatt).
- On this unfair merger, Vallabh Bhai Patel said: "People of Rajasthan wanted Gokul Bhai Bhatt and we have given that."
- Merged on 26th Jan 1950; state was officially named as "Rajasthan".
- Heera Lal Shastri was made 1st Nominated CM of Rajasthan.

Nominated CM of Rajasthan (1950-51 AD):
1. Heera Lal Shastri
2. C.S. Venkatacharya (ICS)
3. Jai Narayan Vyas

Elected CM of Rajasthan:
1. Tika Ram Paliwal
2. Jai Narayan Vyas (Deputy CM: Tikaram Paliwal)
3. Mohanlal Sukhadia - CM for 17 years (1954-1971) - Youngest CM, longest tenure.
4. Barkatullah Khan
5. Haridev Joshi
- In 1st Vidhan Sabha, "Ram Rajya Parishad" (24 Seats) was IInd largest party after Congress.

Phase VII
- Completed on 1st Nov, 1956 on basis of recommendations of State Reorganisation Commission.
- Mergers:
  1. Ajmer-Merwara was merged in Rajasthan.
  2. Abu and Dilwara were merged in Rajasthan (89 villages).
  3. Sunel Tappa of M.P. was merged in Rajasthan.
  4. Sironj of Rajasthan was given to M.P.
- Earlier, Ajmer-Merwara was a Chief Commissionerate and had a Dhara Sabha with 30 members (Hari Bhau Upadhyay was Chief Minister).
- Hari Bhau Upadhyay opposed the merger of Ajmer-Merwara in Rajasthan.
- Dispute between Jaipur and Ajmer for capital. Satya Narayan Rao Committee was formed (Members: Satya Narayan Rao (Chairman), V. Vishwanathan, B.K. Gupta).
- Recommendations of Satya Narayan Rao Committee:
  1. Capital: Jaipur
  2. Revenue Department: Ajmer
  3. Education Department: Bikaner
  4. Agriculture Department: Bharatpur
  5. Forest & Cooperative Department: Kota
  6. Minerals Department: Udaipur
  7. High Court: Jodhpur
  *(Ajmer became the 26th district of Rajasthan)
- Under leadership (Chairmanship) of "Muni Jeen Vijay Suri", a committee was formed (Historian Dashrath Sharma was also a member). On basis of recommendation of this committee, Abu-Dilwara were given to Rajasthan.
- By 7th Constitutional Amendment Act (CAA) 1956: Post of Raj Pramukh was abolished; 1st Governor of Rajasthan was appointed (Sardar Gurumukh Nihal Singh).
- By 26th CAA 1971: Privy purse of kings was abolished.
- On 1st Nov 2000: Rajasthan became the largest state of India area-wise (Chhattisgarh got separated from M.P.).
